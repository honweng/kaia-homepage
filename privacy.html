Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Kaia AI Home Lab ("we", "our", or "us") handles data when you use our local automated tools, including the Gmail Spam Scanner integration.

Information Collection & Use

Our tools interact with your Google Account via official OAuth 2.0 protocols (restricted strictly to gmail.readonly, gmail.modify, and gmail.labels scopes). We process message headers, subjects, and snippets locally on your private homelab server (Raspberry Pi) solely for the purpose of identifying and labeling spam, newsletters, and promotional emails.

Data Storage & Sharing

We do not sell, rent, share, or store your personal email data on external servers. All OAuth credentials and tokens are stored securely in encrypted local configuration files on your private machine. Email contents are evaluated ephemerally and never transmitted to third parties.

Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this privacy policy, you can reach out via our main homepage.
